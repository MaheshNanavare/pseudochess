Privacy policy

PseudoChess. Last updated 25 September 2026.

PseudoChess is a chess game you play against the computer. It does not collect, send or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your own device only, in the browser storage of the app:

  • the moves of the game in progress, your side and the difficulty,
  • your settings (board colours, sound, move hints),
  • whether you have already seen the how-to-play screen.

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party cookies.
  • No network requests during play: the computer opponent runs entirely on your device, and the game works offline.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Questions can be sent through the contact details at maheshnanavare.co.uk.
